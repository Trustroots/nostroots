About & safety

People helping people travel.

Rideshares is an open community noticeboard. It does not verify vehicles, arrange rides, take payment, or guarantee anyone’s identity or behavior.

Keep the meeting point private

Public journeys show only a broad area. Share exact locations after you have talked and feel comfortable.

Use your judgment

Check profiles, tell someone your plans, meet somewhere public, and change your mind whenever something feels wrong.

Open by design

Journey posts live on Nostr relays. You can cancel them, but another relay or client may have retained a copy.
